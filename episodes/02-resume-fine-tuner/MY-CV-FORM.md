## 💡 TIPS FOR FILLING THIS OUT

### DO:
✅ Be specific with technologies and tools
✅ Use numbers and metrics when possible ("increased by 25%")
✅ Include team sizes and project scales
✅ Mention actual companies and products
✅ List skills you can discuss in an interview

### DON'T:
❌ Exaggerate or fabricate experience
❌ List every technology you've ever touched
❌ Use vague descriptions like "helped with" or "assisted in"
❌ Leave dates or companies blank
❌ Include personal information like SSN or marital status
