## ⚠️ IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS

### What to Include:
1. **Be Honest**: Only include experience and skills you actually have
2. **Be Specific**: Use numbers and metrics wherever possible ("increased by 25%" not "improved a lot")
3. **Be Realistic**: Don't exaggerate (20-30% improvements are realistic, 10x is not)
4. **Be Complete**: Include all relevant work history for past 10 years
5. **Be Accurate**: Companies and dates will be verified

### What NOT to Do:
❌ Don't fabricate companies or jobs
❌ Don't claim skills you don't have
❌ Don't make up unrealistic metrics
❌ Don't copy/paste from others' resumes
❌ Don't include outdated tech you no longer remember

### Tips:
✅ Use action verbs: "Built", "Developed", "Led", "Improved"
✅ Include team sizes: "Led team of 5", "Collaborated with 10 engineers"
✅ Include scale: "Processing 1M requests/day", "50K active users"
✅ Include timeframes: "Reduced by 30% over 3 months"
✅ Update this file as you gain more experience

## 🚀 READY TO USE?

Once you've filled this out:

1. **Save this file** as `YOUR-EXPERIENCE.md` or with your name
2. **Find a job posting** you want to apply to and save it as `workspace/job-descriptions/company-role.md`
3. **Run the agent**:
   ```bash
   python resume_fine_tuner.py \
     -e workspace/experience-db/YOUR-EXPERIENCE.md \
     -j workspace/job-descriptions/company-role.md \
     -o workspace/outputs/my-resume.md
   ```

The agent will generate a tailored resume matching your experience to that job!
